# Evidence Boundary

This public repository separates four evidence classes so readers can see what is observed, modeled, directional, or intentionally withheld.

## Verified project findings

The following figures come from the underlying client analysis:

- H1 2026 working baseline: **393.2 accepted appointments/month** from 2,359 accepted appointments across six months
- historical monthly high: **596 accepted appointments** in March 2025
- owner-stated stretch benchmark: **660 appointments/month**
- modeled service requirement at 660 appointments: **~804 service hours/month** using an average appointment duration of about 73.1 minutes
- staffing snapshot: **10 active service providers**
- modeled capacity range: roughly **572 to 1,455 productive hours/month**, depending on recurring weekly coverage assumptions
- 90-day return rate among eligible first-observed customers: **19.8%**

## Modeled estimates

Capacity estimates depend on recurring provider-days and assumed productive hours per provider-day. They are useful for scenario planning, but they are not forecasts or guaranteed output.

## Directional findings

Customer-return analysis is directional because the observed customer history begins in July 2024, and identity matching plus event deduplication affect the denominator and interpretation.

## Working proxies

Accepted appointments were used as a working proxy in the underlying analysis. **Accepted does not mean verified completed.**

## Intentionally withheld

This repository does not include:

- client or customer names
- appointment-level records
- provider schedules or private staffing details
- raw exports
- internal screenshots
- credentials or account information
- confidential financial data

The examples folder uses simplified public-safe scenarios to demonstrate method without publishing private operating data.
